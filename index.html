W-05 · 2025Exhibition · cultural narrative

Hidden Mathematics in the 24 Solar Terms — a Zhoubi Suanjing exhibition.

W-05 / ZHOUBI SUANJING EXHIBITION · 2025

Ancient mathematics as immersive space.

An exhibition translating Zhoubi Suanjing into four spatial chapters: Gougu geometry, shadow measurement, Qiheng diagrams, and Gaitian cosmology. The space uses modular blocks, light, interactive measurement, and astronomical imagery to connect traditional science with contemporary exhibition experience.

W-06 · 2026Landscape · water memory

The flattened river, made visible again.

W-06 / FUHE LOCK REGENERATION · 2026

An abandoned lock as public memory infrastructure.

After the Fuhe River was reorganized as modern park scenery, its working history slipped to the edge. This project takes the abandoned ship lock as its core, using spatial narrative, material contrast, and dynamic routes to re-expose hidden water-transport memory as an urban public experience.
